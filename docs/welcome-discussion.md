**Title:** Welcome to Sakarya Aerospace Engineering Community | Let's Build, Verify & Learn Together

Welcome to the Sakarya Aerospace / SUHAVX open engineering community.

We explore systems engineering, interface verification, change-impact analysis, evidence reuse, simulation, and reproducible engineering methods.

> **When a system changes, what must be verified again — and which existing evidence can still be trusted?**

Students, researchers, developers, and practicing engineers are welcome. Start with the [public repository](https://github.com/HAKOSARI/sakarya-aerospace--engineering-showcase), review our [contribution guide](https://github.com/HAKOSARI/sakarya-aerospace--engineering-showcase/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md), and find a [good first issue](https://github.com/HAKOSARI/sakarya-aerospace--engineering-showcase/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22good+first+issue%22).

**Engineering first. Evidence matters. Respect is mandatory.** No spam, scams, crypto/token promotions, gambling/betting promotions, or unauthorized advertising. We welcome respectful technical disagreement, not harassment or confidential information. See our [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/HAKOSARI/sakarya-aerospace--engineering-showcase/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) once merged.

The public R1–R7 EO-camera demonstrator uses synthetic data and produces advisory recommendations only. It is **not** aircraft qualification, certification, airworthiness, or flight-test evidence.

Introduce yourself, ask a technical question, or contribute a small, testable improvement. Let's build reproducible engineering knowledge together.

**Sakarya Aerospace / SUHAVX**
