# Code of Conduct — Sakarya Aerospace Engineering Community

## Purpose

We maintain a professional, welcoming public engineering environment for students, researchers, developers, and practicing engineers. Participation is voluntary. The same rules apply regardless of experience, employer, nationality, or viewpoint.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss technical claims with assumptions, reproducible examples, and evidence where possible.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people. Be respectful even when disagreeing.
- Welcome questions from beginners and explain technical terms without ridicule.
- Respect privacy, licensing, intellectual property, and security boundaries.
- Keep examples synthetic or authorized for public release; clearly identify limitations.
- Accept maintainer moderation and review decisions; appeals may be made respectfully.

## Unacceptable conduct

- Harassment, hate speech, threats, intimidation, personal attacks, or targeted humiliation.
- Spam, repetitive self-promotion, unsolicited commercial pitches, referral links, or deceptive recruiting.
- Cryptocurrency/token/NFT promotions, investment solicitation, gambling or betting promotions, pump-and-dump schemes, phishing, fraud, or impersonation. Technical discussions of cryptography or relevant distributed-systems engineering are not prohibited merely because they concern those technologies.
- Sharing secrets, credentials, personal data, proprietary/customer data, export-controlled material, or private laboratory information without authorization.
- Fabricated test evidence, misleading certification/qualification claims, or presenting synthetic results as real flight/hardware verification.
- Deliberate disruption, repeated off-topic posts, or attempts to evade moderation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or hide inappropriate posts where GitHub permits, close or lock discussions, remove content, warn participants, and temporarily or permanently restrict participation through available GitHub controls. Severity, repetition, context, and immediate safety risk guide decisions. Severe harassment, fraud, phishing, or sensitive-data exposure may result in immediate restrictions without prior warning. We will apply these standards consistently and explain decisions when safe and practical.

## Reporting

For ordinary conduct concerns, contact repository maintainers through a GitHub issue **without posting private details**. For security vulnerabilities, secrets, or sensitive-data exposure, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and use a private reporting channel if available. Do not disclose sensitive material publicly while reporting.

This policy governs public Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, and other project interactions. It complements GitHub's platform-wide rules.
